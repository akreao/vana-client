## Time-of-day lighting: the zone's `@ini`, fixed sky time, sun tilt, the dead override (2026-11-10)

Modlog-gaps item 132 ("Time-of-day lighting: light yaw world+0x1cc; DAT_1047bf18's override; the writers near
0x10059273 / 0x100604d7; Ambient DAT_1065ca34's source", MODLOG "Not traced: the light yaw world+0x1cc ..."). Addresses
are Steven's FFXiMain.dll, read in the decompile and the disassembly.

- The scheduler's jump table (0x1005dc1c, index op - 2) gives the four ops: 0x82 -> 0x10059273, 0x83 -> 0x10059181,
  0xc0 -> 0x10058079, 0xc3 -> 0x1005809b.
- Every scene load runs the zone file's `@ini` scheduler. FUN_1018b500 (from FUN_1018b3a0: zone-in, scheduler op
  0x7b, the event loaders) first clears the zone object's sky fixes (FUN_1018b620: W+0x1c8 = -1, W+0x1cc = 0), parses
  the file, and ends with FUN_1018b110 -> FUN_1018b8e0: the zone object's vtable (0x10336838) +0x18 = FUN_1018b770
  with ('@ini', 0, 0), which looks a type-7 resource of that name up from the file's root (FUN_10072290) and starts it
  on no actors (FUN_10056c70), stepped at once (FUN_100748c0). So its ops stand before the first light frame.
- Five files have an `@ini` (census `dat::sched::census::light_op_census`, all DATs), each op 01 and one more:
  - op 0xc0, 10:00 (2160000): 83923 Escha - Zi'Tah (scene 732) and 83924 Escha - Ru'Aun (733), under `time`;
  - op 0xc0, 14:00 (3024000): 83928 Reisenjima Sanctorium (737);
  - op 0xc3, 36: 83926 Reisenjima (735) and 83927 Reisenjima Henge (736), under `angl`.
  No other scheduler in any DAT holds op 0xc0 or 0xc3. The earlier note that no zone uses them looked at files
  100..399 only.
- Op 0xc0: W+0x1c8 = u32 @8 through FUN_1018bc40 (above 360 days it wraps once; negative means none). While it is
  not negative, FUN_10187720 (the light) uses it in place of the clock it is handed, and FUN_10189c60 (camera +9
  set, which it is from the title on) hands it to the weather-key lookups and blends (FUN_10189ca0, FUN_10189e30,
  FUN_10189f20, FUN_1018a0f0, FUN_1018a340). So the light's direction and colour, the sky, the fog and the ambient
  stand still at that time; op 0x52's time windows and the element curves keep the real clock (DAT_1065cb18).
- Op 0xc3: W+0x1cc = i16 @8, degrees. FUN_10187720 (0x10187882) builds Rx(degrees x 0.0174528) (FUN_10027b80: m11 =
  cos, m12 = sin, m21 = -sin, m22 = cos) and turns the moon and the sun by it as row vectors (FUN_10028200): (x, y cos
  - z sin, y sin + z cos) with z = 0, so the sun's path leans 36 degrees out of the x / y plane and noon light
  comes in at (0, 0.809, 0.588) instead of straight down. Not a yaw: the earlier name was a guess. The case has no
  break and runs on into case 0x1b (0x100580ab), which looks for an actor named by the same bytes (FUN_100841a0);
  36 is "$" and names none.
- The sun and moon generators (attach modes 0xe / 0xf) take their points from the zone object's +0xb4 / +0xd4
  (FUN_1003be10 through FUN_10186e90 / FUN_10186eb0), which FUN_10187720 sets from the same directions (eye - sun
  x 1000, eye - moon x 900). So the sun sprite stands still in Escha and rides the tilted path in Reisenjima.
- DAT_1047bf18's override is dead. Ops 0x82 / 0x83 make a task (vtable 0x1032b890, update FUN_100604b0, destructor
  FUN_10060590) that blends the zone env's floats @0x50 / @0x54 and bytes @0x5c / @0x5d to the op's values and back,
  through DAT_1065ca54 / ca50 / ca4c / ca48. FUN_10189980 writes those into the record when the flag is set
  (0x101899e8..0x10189a07) and then both paths meet at 0x10189a0a and blend the same four fields from the two keys
  again, so nothing the frame reads changes; the two floats have no reader anyway (item 22). And no scheduler in
  any DAT holds op 0x82 or 0x83. Nothing to build.
- The light's Ambient DAT_1065ca34 has no writer: its only references are the two address-of pushes in
  FUN_10188ca0, and it lies in the zero-filled part of .data (raw size 0x85000 from 0x1034f000). It is 0.
- Ours before: no `@ini`, no ops 0xc0 / 0xc3. The three fixed-time zones followed the clock (night in Escha), and
  Reisenjima's sun went straight over the top.
- Ours now: `dat::weather::Sky` (time, tilt) and `sun_moon_tilted`; `Effects::switch_zone` clears it and runs
  `@ini` at once; scheduler ops 0xc0 / 0xc3 set it; the client's light step takes the minute and the tilt from
  `Effects::sky()` for the weather keys, the eased light, the area lights and the shadow twilight; `sky_point` (sun
  / moon generators) uses the same directions, now with FUN_10187720's stored 6.283 / 3.1415. `zone-shot --hour`
  honours the file's `@ini` (`Sky::of_zone_file`).

  clear at the next load, the sun's point fixed and tilted).
