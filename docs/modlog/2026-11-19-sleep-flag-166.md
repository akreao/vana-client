Modlog-gaps item 166 ("s2c 0x037 Flags0 bits 1 and 3 on our own actor (+0x120 bits 18 and 22, FUN_1009d0f0
0x1009d1d6..); what reads bit 22"). Decompiled with Ghidra; FUN_1008e4d0, FUN_10098210, the writes in FUN_1009d0f0 and
FUN_10094190 and every reader search below checked in Rizin.

- Writers of +0x120 bit 18 (LSB's SleepFlag) and bit 22 (CliPosInitFlag): our own 0x037 (FUN_1009d0f0, Flags0 bits 1
  and 3 at 0x1009d19c / 0x1009d1d7), the zone-in (FUN_10094190 at 0x10094aa3 / 0x10094b18 from the character data
  +0x40e5c bit 12 and +0x40e5e bit 3, which FUN_100faf90 fills from the 0x00A's Flags1 u32 @0x20 bits 2 and 8), and for
  other actors 0x00D (FUN_10099ae0) and 0x00E (FUN_1009b550) from u32 @0x20 bits 2 and 8. The zone-in also writes
  the hide bit 14 (0x00A Flags1 bit 1 -> +0x40e5c bit 11, 0x10094a7f).
- **Bit 22 has no reader.** Searched: `test` / `and` with any mask holding bit 22 and `shr 0x16` / `bt 0x16` in every
  function that touches +0x120 (48 functions with such a mask: only the four writers above and clear masks), and byte
  reads of +0x122 (two in the whole DLL: FUN_1008e4d0's bit 16 test at 0x1008e559 and an unrelated object's +0x122 in
  FUN_101ae860). Nothing to build for it.
- **Bit 18 has two readers.**
  - FUN_1008e4d0 (the per-actor hide pass), its tail at 0x1008e686: for an actor that has not taken part in an event
    (+0x120 bit 8 clear), is not bound to the running one (bit 7 with DAT_104819a5) and is not event-reserved (+0x138
    bit 31), `+0x120 & 0x44000` (the hide bit 14 or the sleep bit 18) sets the unload mark, bit 23, ahead of the
    event's own filter. FUN_10095900 then frees the model of a marked actor (FUN_10092900) unless it is a door, lift
    or ship (FUN_10096200), was made by the client (+0x130 bit 3), is bound or took part (FUN_100961d0: bits 7 / 8)
    or is in use (+0xf2 above 0). The same test earlier in the function (0x1008e52e, for an actor already hidden by
    bits 14 / 15) sets the same mark and changes nothing by itself.
  - FUN_10098210 (may this actor be followed / shown on the compass; callers FUN_1007e190, FUN_100a6240 and
    FUN_102219c0, none for our own actor): no with bit 18. The compass had it already (radar::shown).
  - The event bind (FUN_100bd1a0 -> FUN_1008e4b0) copies bits 14 / 15 only into the script's hide flag, so an asleep
    actor an event binds is loaded and drawn again.
- Ours read the bit for the compass only: an asleep actor without the hide bit was drawn. Now
  `event::unloads` takes it (other actors: `Entity::flags` bit 2; ours: `net::SelfPlate::asleep`), so the model, its
  plate and its targeting go like an event-filtered actor's, and come back when an event binds it.
- Our own hide and sleep bits now also come from the 0x00A at the zone-in (`net::zone_in_plate`, Flags1 bits 1 / 2),
  not only from the first 0x037; `event::self_hidden` reads `SelfPlate::hide`.
- Not built, and so before this change for every unload mark (the event filter, /hidetrust, the hide bit outside an
  event): the model is only not drawn, where retail frees it and loads it again once the mark is gone (FUN_1008f740:
  the load fade from 0, the status motion from the start). The +0xf2 use count and +0x130 bit 3 are not modelled.
  What else our own actor loses while its model is gone (+0xa0 nil) was not walked.
